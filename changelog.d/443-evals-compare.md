### Added

- `shipmill evals compare --base <main.json> --head <pr.json>` gates a pull request's eval run against the default branch's (spec 025, D-37): it counts a run as passed only when every scored grader passed, exits 1 when the suite's pass rate drops more than 0.05 (`--tolerance`), a case falls from 3 of 3 to at most 1 of 3, or a new or `--changed` case passes fewer than 2 of 3, prints each regressed, new failing, improved, and removed case and both pass rates, fails with `evals: budget reached after <n> of <m> runs` on a run stopped at its budget, and exits 2 on a missing or malformed `aggregate-result.json` (#443)
