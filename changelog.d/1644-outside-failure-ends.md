### Changed

- A gate session that ended `ok` although an outside cause stopped its tool calls no longer turns its subject idle (spec 095): the tick reads the session's failed tool results first and records `github_limit` (GitHub's rate limit), `disk` (`No space left on device`), `permission` (a denied tool call, or `permission_denials` in the result), or `error` (a network failure). The first three drop their hand and count no attempt; `github_limit` and `disk` pause every gate on the host through `$XDG_STATE_HOME/shipmill/host/<cause>.json` until `gh api rate_limit` shows room (else its reset, or an hour on) or the checkout's file system has 2 GiB free, shown as `GATE_PAUSED` in `shipmill status` and the watch; `permission` sends one notification per denied tool, recorded in the gate's `denied.json`, and the gate keeps launching (#1644)
